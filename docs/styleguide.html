The language is Claude Code's own usage screen, drawn on a terminal's dark glass. Everything is a line of text in SF Mono 12. Bars are block glyphs. Tables are aligned columns. One accent. The only warm colour is the coral of the fill past the pace tick. No cards, no rounded tiles, no icons in squares, no borders, no shadow except the popover's own.

Source of record: docs.css and docs.js in this folder and the Text module of the Swift package, which draws the same lines. A change to a component is made in both. The observed Claude Code screens the language derives from were captured from Claude Code v2.1.296, theme dark, and the terminal profile is Terminal.app "Clear Dark": SF Mono 12, line spacing 1.0, background rgb(25,29,39) at alpha 0 with window blur.

4 Material

The popover is the terminal's glass: a dark tint over the blurred desktop. On macOS that is an NSVisualEffectView; in these pages it is a painted wallpaper behind a tint. Recipe:

  • tint rgba(25,29,39,.82), blur 24 px behind it
  • popover 380 px wide at 1x, rendered at 2x; padding 12 px 10 px 10 px; radius 10 px
  • shadow: a 1 px hairline at rgba(255,255,255,.1) as a ring, then 0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.45)
  • the arrow under the menu bar item: a 14 px square rotated 45 degrees, ground rgb(30,34,46), the same hairline on its two visible edges
  • no border, no inner shadow, no gradient, no second surface inside the popover; rows and sections sit directly on the glass
  • the menu bar strip, the nudge banner and the menu bar item are OS chrome and keep the OS material (these pages draw them for the stage only)

The tooltip is the one thing with its own ground: the prompt box #373737, text in default colour, one cell of padding, no radius, no shadow.

5 Bar grammar

A bar is a run of block glyphs on a track. It is text: it copies as text, it is read by VoiceOver through its label, and it scales with the font. The sheet closes the hairline SF Mono leaves between two full blocks by painting the full cells' background in the fill colour too.

Cell counts

Fill

For N cells and a used percentage U: f = U / 100 × N, full cells floor(f), then one eighth block for the remainder, round((f - floor(f)) × 8) eighths; eight eighths is a full block. So 61% of 36 cells is 21 full cells and ▊ (0.96 of a cell rounds to eight eighths, a full block: 22 cells). Nothing is drawn fractionally in pixels; the eighth block is the resolution.

The tick

The tick ▏ stands at the elapsed share E of the window: cell min(N - 1, round(E / 100 × N)). It is drawn in text colour #e0e0e0 over the bar, in its own layer, so it shows on fill and on track alike. It is the clock. Rows and Mac bars have no tick: they are shares of a total, not windows of time.

The stale form

Stale means the last snapshot is older than an hour. The fill drops to the track colour, the percent goes dim, the tick goes dim, and the reset line is followed by "as of 13:05" instead of the pace lines. Nothing is projected from old data. The menu bar item dims to 50% in the same state.

6 Components

Every component the popover is built from, in every state it has, with the rule for using it. Hover a figure for its readout; click a checkbox or a section tab to switch it.

6.1 Header line and tab strip

default Claude selected a session feeds
between sessions the snapshot time and its source
Mac selected
with the Mac warning dot
Settings selected

Use once, at the top of the popover. Line one is the updated line, dim: "Usage live" with "Mac live" at the right while a session has fed in the last 60 s; otherwise "Usage as of HH:MM, last Claude Code session" takes the line. There is no plan badge: the tier is not in the feed. Then the ▔ rule in accent across the full width, then the three tabs in Claude Code's tab grammar: the selected one inverted (black on accent, bold), the others in default text, white under the pointer. The Mac tab gets a warning dot ● when memory pressure is warn or critical or one process has been over 80% CPU for 60 s.

Do not add a fourth tab, a title, a name or an icon; the popover has no title. Do not draw a second rule.

6.2 Block bar

See section 5 for the grammar. Use the 36 cell bar for a window of time (it carries the tick), the 24 cell plain bar for a share of a total in a row, the 20 cell bar for a week, the 5 cell bar in the menu bar item. Do not draw a per model limit bar: the documented feed has no such figure. Do not draw a sparkline, a ring or a line chart.

6.3 Window section

live follows the pace switch
weekly over pace
5h window ahead
weekly on pace
weekly stale, as of 13:05

Use for each of the two windows, weekly first, then the 5h window, one blank line between them. Five lines: the title bold; the bar with " N% used" after it; the reset line dim with the clock time and the countdown as the second value; the status word bold followed by the projection dim; the allowance dim. The stale form has three lines: title, bar, reset, then "as of HH:MM".

Do not show a countdown alone, a percent alone, or a projection from stale data. Do not reorder the lines; the eye learns the positions.

6.4 Pace sentence

live
ahead
on pace
over pace
stale

Use under a window bar, three dim lines. The status word is the only bold text in the three lines. The projection has two branches: "limit Sun 01:20 at this rate" when the limit lands before the reset, "76% by reset at this rate" when it does not. The allowance is "0.5% an hour left" (one decimal under 1%, whole numbers above).

Do not join the lines with a separator; two spaces or a line break. Do not colour the status word; the bar's coral already says over pace.

6.5 Table row

models, 7 days Fable selected with ❯
projects, 7 days second row under the pointer
sessions, today
processes by CPU, share of the 9% in use first row under the pointer

Use for a list of named things with a share and a figure: models, projects, sessions, processes. Name column 14 cells (cut with …), one space, a 24 cell plain bar in the family hue, two spaces, the figure right aligned in 5 cells. The bar is the share of the list's total, not of a limit. Hover or focus the figure for the readout tooltip. Row hover is the terminal's selection colour across the full width. A selected row leads with ❯ in accent and its name in bold.

Do not add columns, icons, avatars or a percent next to the figure (the tooltip has it). Do not sort by anything but the figure, largest first. Lists are at most five rows; the rest is "other".

6.6 Section switch

live click to switch
default under the pointer selected

Use under a section title to switch its rows between Models, Projects and Sessions. The sub tab grammar is Claude Code's Overview switch: the selected one inverted in bold colour #ededed with ink #191d27; keys m, p and s switch it.

Do not use it for anything but sibling lists in one section. The main tab strip (6.1) uses the accent inversion; the sub form never does.

6.7 Chip

dim brackets chosen
inverted text not chosen

Use the dim bracketed form for a figure that qualifies a list: "[list price equivalent USD 1,140]" and "[44% by subagents]" under the token rows. The inverted form was shown and rejected: inverted text already means selected, and a chip is not selectable.

Do not put a chip anywhere but directly under the rows it qualifies. Never write "spent".

6.8 Weeks

week over week live current week

Use for week over week: four rows, the date the week started (day and month), a 20 cell bar of the percentage used at the reset or at the limit, then the percent. A week that hit the limit ends in ⚠ in warning and names the day and time it hit, dim. The current week carries the tick and the note "so far", and goes coral past the tick when over pace.

Do not draw more than four weeks (the transcripts keep 30 days by default). Do not colour past weeks by pace: they are finished.

6.9 Memory grid

memory 25.5 of 36 GB pressure normal swap 0.9 GB

Use for the Mac's memory picture, in Claude Code's context grid grammar: 20 by 10 cells of 0.18 GB each on a 36 GB machine, ⛁ used in accent, ⛶ free in dimmer, ⛝ swap in warning, filled left to right, top to bottom, the swap from the end. The legend under it names each glyph with its figure; the pressure level sits after swap.

Do not use the grid for CPU, GPU or battery; those are bars. Do not colour pressure: when it is warn or critical, the word says so and the Mac tab gets its dot.

6.10 Toggle and Settings rows

off on click to toggle
settings rows second row under the pointer

Use the bracket checkbox, the TUI form, for every setting: "[ ]" off, "[x]" on, the label after it, an explanation on a second dim line indented under the label when needed. Rows highlight on hover like table rows. The privacy sentence stands as two dim lines. Quit is the last row with ⌘Q right aligned.

Do not use the macOS switch (it is not text) or ● and ○ (● is taken by the Mac warning dot). Do not add settings beyond the three in the spec without a decision. The status line entry row is an action, not a setting: "Install the status line entry" or "Remove the status line entry", one click each, with what it does on a dim second line.

6.11 Tooltip

Use one item. The panda from the app icon as a 7 by 7 pixel mark, a gap of 5 px, then the weekly percentage in SF with tabular digits, monochrome like Apple's own items (a template image, so it inverts on a light menu bar). 6.13 Nudge banner

6.14 Open session

Use under the two windows, one entry per Claude Code session that has fed in the last 10 s. Line one: the project folder's name in bold, the model dim, the context figure "405.6k of 1M" dim at the right. Line two: the 36 cell bar of the context window with " 41% used". No tick: a context window is a share, not a time window. The figure's tooltip carries the list price equivalent and the lines added and removed. Largest context first, at most five.

Do not draw a breakdown of the context by category or per MCP tool; those figures are not in the feed. Do not keep a session on the list more than 60 s after its last feed. Omit the section when no session is open.

7 Copy rules

  • Status words, exactly: "Ahead", "On pace", "Over pace". Bold, at the start of the status line, nowhere else.
  • The reset line: "Resets" then the clock time, then two spaces, then the countdown. Weekly: "Resets Sun 18:00 in 3d 3h". 5h window: "Resets 17:45 in 3h 25m". The weekday is three letters; the clock is 24 hour with a leading zero in the minutes.
  • Clock times, never a countdown alone. A countdown may follow a clock time as the second value. "in 1d 13h" on its own is banned.
  • Projection: "limit Sun 01:20 at this rate" or "76% by reset at this rate". Times are rounded to five minutes.
  • Allowance: "0.5% an hour left". One decimal under 1%, whole numbers from 1% up.
  • The percent: " 61% used" after the bar, one space before. "used" is lower case.
  • Updated line: "Usage live" with "Mac live" at the right while a session feeds; "Usage as of 13:05, last Claude Code session" between sessions. Stale sections say "as of 13:05" under the reset line, and the stale Claude tab adds "Limits are shared across Claude and Claude Code, so use elsewhere moves these figures."
  • Context figure: "405.6k of 1M", k and M with one decimal, the window size without one.
  • "list price equivalent", never "spent", never "cost you". The chip reads "[list price equivalent USD 1,140]". The setting's second line: "tokens at list prices, not the plan price".
  • Agent share: "[44% by subagents]".
  • No separators. No middle dot, no pipe, no em or en dash anywhere, in the UI or in the docs. Two spaces between values on a line, or a line break. Claude Code uses the middle dot; this app does not.
  • No centred text. Everything starts at the left cell. Figures are right aligned in their column, which is alignment, not centring.
  • No emoji, no sparkles, no "AI" as decoration.
  • Sentence case for titles and labels: "Weekly (all models)", "Tokens by model 7 days", "Week over week", "Processes by CPU", "Mac figures in the bar".
  • Units: tokens as "72.4M", "2.1B"; memory "25.5 of 36 GB (71%)"; power "47.6 W in 94 W adapter"; time "full in 51 min", "health 89%", "316 cycles"; CPU "user 5% system 4% 10 cores".
  • The privacy sentence in Settings, verbatim: "This app makes no network calls. Usage comes from Claude Code's own status feed on this Mac."
  • Key hints in the foot, quiet: "Esc closes Tab switches m p s sections" on the Claude tab, "Esc closes Tab switches" on the others.
  • Unknown window: a feed key the app does not know prints "unknown window" in place of the pace lines; nothing is guessed.
  • Names: "Claude Code", "5h window", "weekly" are facts and may be used. The app's name, tokenpanda, is never drawn in the popover.

8 Motion

Two moments, nothing else moves.

  • The popover opens: opacity 0 to 1 and scale .96 to 1 from the arrow, 150 ms, ease-out. Closing is the same in reverse.
  • The bars fill once to their value when a tab renders: a clip from the left, 240 ms, ease-out; the tick is already in place and the fill arrives under it.

The tooltip fades in over 150 ms. The menu bar item never animates. Nothing bounces, nothing loops, nothing pulses.

Reduced motion. With "Reduce motion" on, every transition and animation is off and the finished state is shown at once. In CSS the animations live inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference); in Swift, read NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion and skip both moments.

Keyboard

  • Open and close the popover from the menu bar item; Esc closes it.
  • Tab moves between the three tabs; Shift Tab goes back. Inside a tab, the figures and the checkboxes are in the tab order in reading order.
  • m, p, s switch the token section on the Claude tab.
  • Space toggles a focused checkbox.
  • ⌘Q quits.
  • The focus ring is a 1 px accent outline with 1 px offset, custom, never the default blue.

Text size follows the system monospace font; the layout is cells, so it holds at any size.
